Wheel Bolt Engineering
02 / BOLTS

Wheel Fastener Requirements

Wheel fastener selection depends on the vehicle manufacturer specification, wheel design, hub interface, fastener grade and required installation torque. Higher vehicle speeds increase the importance of correct wheel installation and inspection.

Windshield Engineering
03 / WINDSHIELD

Laminated Safety Glazing

Automotive windshields use laminated safety glazing designed to maintain structural integrity and provide occupant protection. High-speed aerodynamic loading and glazing bonding are important engineering considerations.

Safety Engineering
04 / SAFETY

Speed Changes Vehicle Energy

Vehicle kinetic energy increases with the square of speed. Consequently, braking, tyre grip, suspension performance and aerodynamic stability become increasingly important as target speed rises.

🌿
Eco-Efficiency
05 / ECO

Aerodynamic Energy Demand

Aerodynamic drag increases strongly with vehicle speed. At higher speeds, maintaining velocity requires substantially more energy, which can increase fuel consumption or battery energy demand.
